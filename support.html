开始使用

打开 SquatPulse 后，选择“相机模式”或“口袋模式”。相机模式需要将身体置于画面中；口袋模式需要允许“运动与健身”权限，并将 iPhone 稳定放置在口袋中。完成训练后，可在历史页面查看本机保存的训练摘要。

权限问题

如果相机模式无法启动，请前往 iPhone“设置”→“SquatPulse”并允许“相机”。如果口袋模式不可用，请在相同位置允许“运动与健身”。权限被拒绝时，对应模式不会运行；你仍可查看已保存在本机的历史记录。

计数说明

SquatPulse 提供一般训练辅助。相机角度、光照、衣着、运动幅度、口袋位置及设备方向都可能影响识别结果。它不提供医疗、诊断、康复评估或专业运动建议。

SquatPulse Support

Getting started

Open SquatPulse and choose Camera mode or Pocket mode. Camera mode needs your body positioned in the camera view. Pocket mode needs Motion & Fitness permission and works best when your iPhone is placed steadily in a pocket. Completed-workout summaries are available in the local History view.

Permission help

If Camera mode cannot start, go to iPhone Settings → SquatPulse and allow Camera. If Pocket mode is unavailable, allow Motion & Fitness in the same location. Denying a permission prevents the related mode from running but does not stop you from viewing history stored on the device.

Counting notice

SquatPulse is a general training aid. Camera angle, lighting, clothing, range of motion, pocket placement, and device orientation can affect detection. It does not provide medical, diagnostic, rehabilitation, or professional fitness advice.
