SquatPulse Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026 · Applies to SquatPulse 2.0 (build 9) and later, on iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch, including widgets, Live Activities and Siri shortcuts · First published: August 26, 2026

中文版（蹲咚）

In short: SquatPulse has no account, no server, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Camera frames and sensor readings are processed on your device and never uploaded. Your workout data stays on your device unless you choose to export it, share it, or write workouts to Apple Health.

1. Scope

This policy applies to the SquatPulse app for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch, and to its Home Screen, Lock Screen and Dynamic Island widgets, Live Activities, and Siri / Shortcuts actions. SquatPulse counts squats with Pocket mode (motion sensors) or Camera mode (on-device pose detection), and offers workout history, challenges, a companion game, a two-player squat-off, and optional Pro features.

SquatPulse is a standalone app. It does not create an account, does not connect to a service operated by us, and has no cloud sync.

2. Who is responsible

SquatPulse is made by an independent developer (“we”, “us”). We do not operate a server that receives your data, so we cannot access, read or restore anything stored on your device. For any privacy question or request, write to genweimi@gmail.com; we reply within 15 working days.

4. Apple Health in detail

  • Write-only. The app requests permission to save workouts and reads nothing from Apple Health. There is no read permission to grant, and health data is never used for advertising or analytics.
  • iPhone: the saved workout contains the activity type (strength training), start time, end time and duration. Rep counts, heart rate and calories are not written.
  • Apple Watch: the saved workout contains the same fields, plus the rep count of that session stored as a custom metadata field on the workout. The Health app does not display this field, but it is kept with the workout in your Health database and is visible to apps you grant access to.
  • Screen-off counting on iOS 26: the workout session exists only to keep counting while the screen is locked. It does not create a Health workout by itself and does not read health data.
  • If you decline: nothing is written to Health, and Pocket workouts pause when the device locks.

5. Information you choose to share

Data leaves your device only when you start it yourself:

  • CSV export: creates a file with the date, squats, duration, mode, count feedback and sets of each workout, saved to a temporary folder and opened in the system share sheet. Where it goes is up to you; the temporary file is managed by the system.
  • Share card: renders an image of your workout result and opens the system share sheet. It is not saved to Photos unless you choose to save it there.
  • Feedback email: opens your mail app with a draft that includes the app version, system version and your selected training mode. You can edit or delete anything before sending. We use emails only to reply to you.
  • Links: the privacy policy, support page, terms and App Store pages open in Safari or the App Store and are governed by those services.

6. No account, ads, analytics or tracking

SquatPulse does not require an account and contains no advertising SDK, no third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDK, and no cross-app tracking. It does not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA), does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission, and does not include social login or Game Center. There is no server of ours to receive your data, and we do not sell, rent or share your information.

Because we operate no server, there is no transfer of your data across borders by us. If you use iCloud or computer backups, the app’s local data is included in those backups under Apple’s settings and policies; that is not a transfer to us.

8. Where your data is kept and for how long

Workout history, settings, game progress and widget summaries are stored in the app’s sandbox on your device, plus a shared container for widgets and the Watch app. They are kept until you delete the item or delete the app; we keep no copy. Exported CSV files live in a temporary folder and are cleaned up by the system. Data inside device backups follows your backup settings.

9. Deleting your data

  • Delete a single workout in History, or a custom challenge by long-pressing it.
  • Turning off Apple Health stops new workouts from being written. Workouts already saved there can be managed or deleted in the Health app.
  • Deleting the app removes all of its local data from your device, including widget and Watch data. Copies inside device backups follow your backup settings.

Because there is no account or server, we cannot see, retrieve or delete your data remotely — and nobody else can through us.

10. Your rights

Your data is on your device, so you can view, correct (edit a workout), export (CSV) and delete it directly in the app at any time. For any request about your personal information, email us and we will reply within 15 working days.

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, you also have the right to object to processing, to restrict it, and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. If you are a California resident, we do not sell or share your personal information as those terms are defined by the CCPA/CPRA, and we do not knowingly collect information from consumers under 16.

11. Children

SquatPulse is a general workout aid, not a service directed to children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the minimum age in your region).

12. Security

The app relies on the iOS sandbox and on your device’s encryption. Because there is no server of ours and no account, there is no online profile of you to breach. Keep a device passcode enabled, and check which app you are sharing an export or share card with before you send it.

13. Changes to this policy

If we ever add an account, cloud sync, analytics, advertising or any other feature that changes how data is handled, we will update this policy, the in-app privacy page and the App Store privacy details before that feature is released.

Changes in 2.0: this version adds coverage for the Apple Watch app, the companion game, the two-player squat-off, Pro and custom challenges, and Siri / Shortcuts. It also states precisely which fields are written to Apple Health on iPhone and on Apple Watch.

4. Apple 健康的具体说明

  • 只写不读。本应用只申请写入体能训练的权限，不读取 Apple 健康里的任何数据；没有可授予的读取权限，健康数据也不会用于广告或统计。

  • iOS 26 的熄屏计数：体能训练会话只用于锁屏后继续计数，本身不会生成健康记录，也不读取健康数据。
